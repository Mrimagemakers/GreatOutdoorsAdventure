Asset credits

Pine trees pack

Pine trees pack (lowpoly, game ready, LODs) by LOLIPOP.

Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

Modifications: extracted two LOD2 trees, removed unused geometry and textures, changed foliage to alpha masking, normalized tree scale and centering, and added runtime instancing, wind and seasonal snow.

Ground textures

Dry Ground Rocks (dirt) and Asphalt Snow (winter road) from Poly Haven, released under CC0.

Modifications: colour and OpenGL normal maps resized to 1024 px and recompressed. Normal maps for the grass, dry grass, gravel, rock, snow and asphalt colour textures were generated from those textures for this game.

Wildlife

The deer, elk, black bear, red fox, hare and coyote are original procedural models and animation written for this game.

Character bodies and hatchet

Marcus Reed and Hank Doyle stand on the rigged lumberjack body, and the hatchet is the lumberjack pack's hatchet, both from the lumberjack_axe pack the project owner supplied. Jamal Okafor stands on the rigged Blake body, also supplied by the project owner. Original authors and licence terms were not included with the files. Add them here before you publish.

Modifications: converted from FBX to glTF; removed animations, blend shapes, the prop gun and the original head; merged the parts into one mesh; resized the textures to 1024 px; recoloured the clothing and skin per character in the game. Each man keeps his own procedural head and painted face. Our own pose rig drives the bodies.

Characters, weapons and vehicle cards

The three women playable characters (Elena, Aiyana and Mei), all six characters' heads and faces, and the seven weapon models are original procedural models written for this game; the weapon set follows the supplied weapon reference sheet. The catalog vehicle thumbnails are cut out from the supplied vehicle reference sheet (some recoloured) and placed over the game's garage backdrop. The named vehicles still drive with the included truck or buggy 3D models.

Three.js
