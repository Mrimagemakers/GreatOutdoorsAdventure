Asset credits

Pine trees pack

Pine trees pack (lowpoly, game ready, LODs) by LOLIPOP.

Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

Modifications: extracted two LOD2 trees, removed unused geometry and textures, changed foliage to alpha masking, normalized tree scale and centering, and added runtime instancing, wind and seasonal snow.

Ground textures

Dry Ground Rocks (dirt) and Asphalt Snow (winter road) from Poly Haven, released under CC0.

Modifications: colour and OpenGL normal maps resized to 1024 px and recompressed. Normal maps for the grass, dry grass, gravel, rock, snow and asphalt colour textures were generated from those textures for this game.

Wildlife

The deer, elk, black bear, red fox, hare and coyote are original procedural models and animation written for this game.

Three.js

three.js r185 with GLTFLoader, BufferGeometryUtils and post-processing passes, bundled locally in vendor/. MIT License, © three.js authors.
