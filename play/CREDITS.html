Asset credits

Pine trees pack

Pine trees pack (lowpoly, game ready, LODs) by LOLIPOP.

Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

Modifications: extracted two LOD2 trees, removed unused geometry and textures, changed foliage to alpha masking, normalized tree scale and centering, and added runtime instancing, wind and seasonal snow.

Ground textures

Dry Ground Rocks (dirt) and Asphalt Snow (winter road) from Poly Haven, released under CC0.

Modifications: colour and OpenGL normal maps resized to 1024 px and recompressed. Normal maps for the grass, dry grass, gravel, rock, snow and asphalt colour textures were generated from those textures for this game.

Wildlife

The deer, elk, black bear, red fox, hare and coyote are original procedural models and animation written for this game.

Character bodies

All six characters are built from the MakeHuman base mesh, its body-shape targets and its default skeleton and weights. These assets are released under CC0. A build script written for this game sets each person's sex, age, build, ancestry and height. It merges the skeleton into a 76-bone game rig with full fingers, and paints each person's skin, face, brows, beard, hair and clothes.

Motion capture

The idle, walk and run animations are Mixamo captures taken from the X Bot sample in the three.js examples. At runtime they are retargeted onto each character. Mixamo animations may be used royalty-free in games and projects (Adobe Mixamo terms). They are included here only as part of the game and are not offered as separate assets.

Deer and elk

Deer and elk use “Realistic Deer 3D Model 2.0 (Demo Free Download)” by WildMesh 3D, licensed under CC BY 4.0. The project owner supplied the file.

Changes: the animation, tangents and extra UV sets were removed. The materials were converted from specular-glossiness to metal-roughness, and the textures were re-encoded as JPEG. In the game the model is scaled to each species. Deer get a recoloured tan coat and smaller antlers on bucks, and antlers are hidden on cows and does. The game's own movement drives the skeleton.

Hatchet

The hatchet model comes from the lumberjack_axe pack supplied by the project owner. Its original author and licence terms were not included with the files. Add them here before you publish. Modifications: converted to glTF and resized the textures.

Characters, weapons and vehicle cards

The hats, the braid and bun, the fallback bodies shown while the character models load, and the seven weapon models are original procedural models written for this game; the weapon set follows the supplied weapon reference sheet. The catalog vehicle thumbnails are cut out from the supplied vehicle reference sheet (some recoloured) and placed over the game's garage backdrop. The named vehicles still drive with the included truck or buggy 3D models.

Three.js

three.js r185 with GLTFLoader, BufferGeometryUtils and post-processing passes, bundled locally in vendor/. MIT License, © three.js authors.
